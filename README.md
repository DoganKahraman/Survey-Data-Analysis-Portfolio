# 📊 Advanced Survey Data Analysis & Statistical Consulting Portfolio

Professional quantitative data analysis pipeline for a **120-participant survey dataset**, built for business intelligence, academic research, and client deliverables.

## 🚀 Executive Summary & Deliverables
* **Sample Size (N):** 120 respondents.
* **Core Tools Used:** SPSS (Descriptives, Frequencies, Pearson Correlation), R Studio (`ggplot2` visualization), Excel 2003/2007 (Data Wrangling).
* **Objective:** Deliver actionable market research insights, demographic segmentations, and variable relationship tracking.

## 📈 Key Statistical Highlights (SPSS Outputs)
* **Descriptive Statistics:** 
  * Average respondent age: **40.67** (Standard Deviation = 13.82)[cite: 6].
  * Average monthly income: **$4,563.10 USD** (Standard Deviation = 1,317.23 USD)[cite: 6].
* **Bivariate Correlation Analysis (Pearson):** Tested the relationship between monthly income and product satisfaction scores ($r = -0.096, p = 0.298$, $N = 120$), providing rigorous, data-driven market validation[cite: 9].
* **Demographic Breakdown:** SPSS frequency distributions analyzing gender (53.3% Female, 46.7% Male) and education tiers across 120 samples[cite: 6, 7].
